During the Beacon endpoint-detection incident the threat-triage rollup that decides which host detections warrant responder escalation began emitting an unreliable queue, so genuine intrusions across the endpoint fleet are going uncontained. Endpoint threat detection is a security control, and the restored system must reflect the incident review board's containment rulings exactly, not any reasonable-looking scheme of its own. A reconciler at `/app/workflow/export_report.py` turns the raw endpoint detections into the triage artifacts responders act on — `summary.json`, `service_matrix.json`, and the compact JSON-lines escalation queue `flagged.jsonl` — and you must **create** an audit tool at `/app/threat_audit.py` — it does not exist yet — exposing two subcommands: `diagnose --dossier PATH --report PATH`, which reads the review log at `--dossier` and writes its diagnosis to the `--report` output path it creates, and `repair --output-dir PATH`. `diagnose` emits a stateless report of the six known containment defects — `wrong_source_field`, `risk_threshold_filter`, `recency_order`, `risk_class_normalization`, `dedupe_event`, and `benign_filter` — each with its evidence, writing a complete diagnosed report on every explicit call whether or not a repair has already run; `repair` rebuilds the rollup from the frozen snapshot, runs it, and writes `diagnosis.json` and `repair_audit.json` beside the three artifacts, defaulting to `/app/output`.

Every governing value behind the triage — severity normalization and alias handling, `detection_id` deduplication tie-breaks, the suppression-override windows and their scopes, the threat-chain correlation, the reach propagation across chains, and the sequential escalation-pressure ledger with its ceilinged carry credit and floored decay — was settled in the board's ticketed decisions recorded in the dossier at `/app/incident/export_dossier.md`, the authoritative record where a later dated ruling overrides an earlier one. Most of that log is routine noise and several early positions were revised more than once, so trace each rule to its final decision rather than the first one you find. The exact schemas, key sets, field types, the per-issue evidence substrings under `required_terms_by_issue`, the `repair_audit` processing steps, digest payloads, and byte-level checksum serialization are fixed by the contract at `/app/docs/report_spec.json`, with the implementation guide at `/app/docs/output_contract.md`. For each diagnosed defect, `dossier_quote` and `pipeline_evidence` must each be an exact, character-for-character substring copied straight from its source file — the dossier and the frozen `/app/workflow/.export_report.original` respectively — never paraphrased, re-wrapped or summarised; only `repair_action` is composed, and it too must contain its required terms exactly as the spec lists them. The read-only frozen snapshot at `/app/workflow/.export_report.original` supplies the pre-repair SHA-256 and the forbidden source tokens the repair must remove, and must remain unmodified. The repaired rollup is graded on behaviour: it must accept an alternate detection stream via `--input`, produce identical output on reruns, and derive every emitted value from the operational inputs and the governing decisions.
